Terms of use for TIPMIP data
============================

These Terms of Use govern the use of all TIPMIP data products, and specify the conditions users of these products
must adhere to.

By downloading any TIPMIP dataset from either DKRZ or an ESGF node, you agree to the following:

    1. You acknowledge that any and all TIPMIP data is publicly available and is provided with no guarantees of
    its accuracy, correctness, or suitability for any particular purpose.

    2. You agree that no individual or institution associated with TIPMIP can be held liable for any errors in the
    data provided, or for any consequence resulting from their use.

    3. You agree to limit your use to the license conditions applicable to each dataset. Most datasets are licensed
    under the Creative Commons CC0 license. Some datasets however may be released under a different
    license. In that case, this will be specified on the TIPMIP website. The license for a dataset can also be
    found within the metadata of each file.

    4. You agree to cite the datasets used in your publications by using their corresponding DOIs. All modelling
    groups/centres that provided data used in the analysis must be offered at least one co-authorship. More
    are possible, at the discretion of the lead author.

    5. You agree to credit TIPMIP in your publications by citing
    1) the `TIPMIP framework paper <https://egusphere.copernicus.org/preprints/2025/egusphere-2025-1899/>`_,
    and 2) by citing the experiment protocol for the corresponding domain or domains from which you are using data
    (e.g. the `TIPMIP-ESM protocol <https://egusphere.copernicus.org/preprints/2025/egusphere-2025-3604/>`_).

    6. You agree to notify the TIPMIP team when publishing any scientific article based on TIPMIP data, and to
    have this publication registered in the TIPMIP publication database. Please notify us before submission so
    that a numerical identifier for your publication can be generated.

    7. You agree to formally acknowledge the institutional facilitators of TIPMIP by including the following
    statement in your publications:

        We acknowledge the Tipping Points Modelling Intercomparison Project (TIPMIP) and its participants
        for producing and publishing their simulation data. We thank the Max-Planck Institute of Geoanthropology
        (MPI-GEA) and the Potsdam Institute for Climate Impact Research

        (PIK) for coordinating TIPMIP and providing the infrastructure necessary to advance the project.
        We also thank the many people and institutions behind the Coupled Model Intercomparison
        Project (CMIP) and the Earth System Grid Federation (ESGF) for their guidance and support
        of initiatives such as TIPMIP. This is TIPMIP contribution #XX.


    8. You agree to acknowledge the use of DKRZ resources by adding the following to your publications:

    This work used resources of the Deutsches Klimarechenzentrum (DKRZ) granted by its Scientific Steering
    Committee (WLA) under project 1448.

    9. If the datasets used fall within either CMIP6Plus or CMIP7, you are also bound by the
    `CMIP terms of use <https://pcmdi.llnl.gov/CMIP6Plus/TermsOfUse/TermsOfUse6-5.html>`_.
